About Cedarline

Fewer, better things for the home.

Cedarline started with a simple frustration: home essentials in Lebanon were either cheap and short-lived, or beautiful and impossible to find. We wanted the middle — honest, well-made pieces at fair prices, delivered to your door.

Every product in our edit is chosen for how it performs in a real home: a cutting board that survives a family lunch, towels that dry in a humid coastal summer, storage that makes a small apartment feel bigger. We favour natural and refillable materials, and we test everything ourselves before it reaches the shop.

We’re a small team based in Beirut. We pack every order by hand, and we answer every message ourselves.
