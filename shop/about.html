About Cedarline

Better tools for Lebanese kitchens.

Cedarline started with a simple frustration: kitchenware in Lebanon was either cheap and short-lived, or beautiful and impossible to find. We wanted the middle — honest, well-made pieces at fair prices, delivered to your door.

Every product is chosen for how it performs in a real Lebanese kitchen: a pan that handles a family breakfast, a casserole for a slow Sunday stew, a mezze set for guests, storage that makes a small apartment kitchen feel bigger. We test everything ourselves before it reaches the shop.

We’re a small team based in Beirut. We pack every order by hand, and we answer every message ourselves.
