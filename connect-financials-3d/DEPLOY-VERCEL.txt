PUT CONNECT FINANCIALS ONLINE WITH VERCEL (free, about 5 minutes)
==================================================================

You get a public https link (like connect-financials.vercel.app). Open it in
Chrome on your laptop or phone, allow the microphone, and talk to Valgon.
Your keys are stored in Vercel's settings, never in the page.

1. Go to https://vercel.com and click "Sign Up" > "Continue with GitHub"
   (use the GitHub account that owns rankrise44-svg/rank-rise). Pick the free
   "Hobby" plan.

2. Click "Add New..." > "Project". Find "rank-rise" and click "Import"
   (if it is not listed, click "Adjust GitHub App Permissions" and allow
   the rank-rise repository).

3. On the "Configure Project" screen:
   - Project Name: connect-financials (this becomes your link)
   - Root Directory: click "Edit" and choose  connect-financials-3d
   - Framework Preset: Vite (it fills in by itself)
   - Open "Environment Variables" and add two:
        Name: GROQ_API_KEY         Value: your Groq key
        Name: ELEVENLABS_API_KEY   Value: your ElevenLabs key
   - Click "Deploy". This first try may fail: it uses the repository's main
     branch, which holds your Rank Rise site, not this one. That is fine.

4. Point Vercel at the Connect Financials branch:
   - Open the project > "Settings" > "Environments" > "Production"
     (on older screens: "Settings" > "Git" > "Production Branch").
   - Set the branch to:   claude/nifty-maxwell-po5fhr   and save.

5. Deploy that branch:
   - Go to "Deployments" > "Create Deployment" (top right), type
     claude/nifty-maxwell-po5fhr  and click "Create Deployment".
   - Wait about a minute until it says "Ready".

6. Click "Visit" (or open https://connect-financials.vercel.app). Click the
   blue microphone next to Valgon's text box and choose "Allow".

From now on, every change pushed to that branch goes online by itself.

IF SOMETHING IS WRONG
- Valgon only answers with website options: the GROQ_API_KEY variable is
  missing or wrong. Fix it in Settings > Environment Variables, then
  Deployments > the latest one > "..." > "Redeploy".
- Robot browser voice: the ElevenLabs key is missing or its account is
  blocked. Everything else still works.
- Build failed: open the failed deployment, copy the red error lines, and
  send them to Claude.
